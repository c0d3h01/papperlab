Account

Sign in
with Google.

One consent connects your Google account for Drive import and save. PaperLab creates no account and keeps no profile on its server.

What Google shares

Two permissions, clearly bounded.

  • Your name, email and photo Shown in the account menu. Never sent to the PaperLab API.
  • Files you pick or PaperLab creates The drive.file scope cannot list or read the rest of your Drive.
  • Nothing else in your Drive No full-Drive access, no background sync, no document library.
What stays in this tab

No PaperLab account. No server session.

The Google token and display profile live in sessionStorage. Disconnecting revokes the token and clears both.
